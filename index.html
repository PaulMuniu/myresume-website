About

I started in data analysis and education, asking why some numbers move and others don't. Now I build the tools that answer those questions.

Most dashboards stop at "what happened". I like to get to "what should we do next". That idea runs through SomaFlow, a platform I designed and shipped end to end. It assesses a student, finds their weakest topic, and recommends a practice plan.

I'm comfortable across the whole pipeline: cleaning and modelling data, writing SQL, building Power BI and Streamlit reports, and shipping web apps with React, Django REST Framework and PostgreSQL.
