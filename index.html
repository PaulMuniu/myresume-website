About

I came to data science from business IT and banking. At KCB, spotting trends in customer data helped lift quarterly sales by 15%, and I've been chasing that kind of result ever since.

I hold a BTech in Business Information Technology from the Technical University of Kenya and trained as a data scientist at Moringa School. I care about the step most dashboards skip: what should we do next? That question drives SomaFlow, a learning platform I designed and shipped end to end.

I work across the whole pipeline: cleaning data and building ETL, exploratory analysis, machine-learning models, Power BI and Tableau dashboards, and web apps with React, Django REST Framework and PostgreSQL.
